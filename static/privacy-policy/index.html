Privacy Policy / Política de Privacidade

History Sanitizer Browser Extension

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Overview

History Sanitizer is a privacy-focused browser extension that helps users clean their browsing history, URL suggestions, and bookmarks. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect any data.

History Sanitizer operates entirely on your local device. No data is:

  • Sent to external servers
  • Collected for analytics
  • Shared with third parties
  • Tracked or monitored
  • Stored remotely

How It Works

  1. Local Processing: All operations happen on your device
  2. No Network Requests: The extension never connects to the internet
  3. Local Storage Only: Settings are stored using the browser’s local storage API
  4. No Background Sync: No data synchronization to external services

Data Usage

What We Access

  • Browsing History: To delete URLs matching your filter criteria
  • Bookmarks: To remove bookmarks matching your filter criteria
  • Storage: To save your extension settings locally

How We Use It

  • Filtering and deleting matching URLs from your history
  • Removing matching bookmarks
  • Storing your preferences (blocked words, intervals, etc.)

Data Storage

  • All settings are stored locally in your browser
  • No data leaves your device
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the extension

Third-Party Services

We do NOT use any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Cloud storage
  • CDN services
  • Tracking pixels

Children’s Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the extension’s repository.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Open Source

History Sanitizer is open source. You can review the complete source code to verify our privacy practices.

Summary: History Sanitizer is a completely offline, privacy-focused tool. We don’t collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your device.
